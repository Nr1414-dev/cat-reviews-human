Entry for [Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-cat-reviews-its-42a34990) — a $2 make.fun bounty, top 10 paid places.

`main.py` writes one funny single-line one-star review from a house cat about its human (under 200 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`:

> One star. He put a cucumber behind me while I ate. I jumped six feet straight up. He laughed. The cucumber is now my mortal enemy, and he is next.

The line was picked from 8 candidates scored against the judge's brief, then checked 1v1 against the field: "cucumber" scores 0 hits across all 543 field titles, while the usual cat-review angles (food bowl, keyboard, sunbeam, 3 a.m.) are saturated.
